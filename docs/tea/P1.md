## Evidence and conventions

The [Mana Notion guide](https://app.notion.com/p/The-Epic-of-Alexander-Ultimate-TEA-226507c362a280e39a49c7406fcafe3c) was read in the browser, including its waymark image and linked guide descriptions. Assignment authority is the [JP macro](https://jp.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/34120564/blog/5274750/), [Fuccdayo guide](https://fuucdayo.com/battle/3685/) and [P1 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxy_ZYEYwlE). All eleven article diagrams were visually inspected; downloaded Japanese automatic captions corroborate the prose at 02:04–06:19. Captions misrecognize several mechanic names and do not establish exact timing.

The supplied two clear recordings show four unhit, stationary dolls and a first Hand of Pain despite more than 24 percentage points of boss HP disparity in one sampled pre-hit state. Their observed first Hand of Pain actions are at pull-relative 42.073 and 42.066 seconds. Both have combinations of Hand debuffs, party mitigation and shields. Reported post-mitigation/shield damage ranges approximately 1,300–23,400; these numbers do not reveal the raw HP-difference formula.

Mana defines north as the lone spout, opposite the open side. D3 baits that spout, D4 the relative western spout, H1 the eastern. This is a strategy coordinate transformation; the generic encounter's `TornadoT` still describes the physical spouts from their open side. Numeric walking coordinates approximate the guide's visual arena rings and are tuned for the engine's hitboxes, rather than claimed as measured retail positions.

MT pulls Living Liquid toward the open southern edge. ST separates the hand northwest of center to force the palm/Parting tell, then MT provokes it after the tell. MT uses invulnerability for the first combined tankbusters. After visible cones lock, H1/D4 bait their unseen cones farther south and away from the spouts; D3 crosses the center and baits the north cone toward the south. Other members assemble behind the bosses. Hand debuff mitigation precedes the unequal-HP Hand of Pain; the barrier follows the unseen Protean.

After the orbs start moving, MT kites the bosses around the outer southern arc toward the relative western spout. ST then provokes the hand back and establishes a separate buster facing. The party follows behind both bosses, clear of orb lines and doll Exhausts. Dolls are neither attacked nor fed; an actually attacked doll still fixates and chases normally.

## Damage and support limitations

The unequal-HP Hand of Pain uses an explicitly **unverified practice threshold of 35% effective mitigation**, combining the caster's modeled debuff mitigation with party mitigation and a prepared barrier. That number is tuning for a meaningful mitigation/barrier exercise, not a measured retail raw-damage formula or exact HP simulation. Every seat, including bots, is checked; a missing barrier can kill a bot. The inherited equal-HP/full-timeline Hand of Pain remains its original coarse action.

Party support currently uses native status preparation, similar to the existing Crystallize Time practice approximation. This does not reproduce every healer spell, potency, range, party job combination or healing rotation. Resolved local Succor/Adloquium and Eukrasian Prognosis/Diagnosis now apply timed barriers to their actual recipients; Temperance and the three ranged party mitigation buttons apply their party statuses. Barriers share the existing fixed 20,000 HP practice approximation, not spell potency or gear scaling. Temperance is a radius-limited application at resolution, not a continuously refreshed aura. Focused healer/ranged tests invoke the real resolved-effect table and inspect recipients, range and refresh behavior. Native MP/gauge costs, cast input/completion and manual healer-button timing remain native acceptance checks; the headless fixture cannot execute the full native cost handler. Tankbuster lethality outside the tank role, cone overlap, water vulnerability, orb collision and doll feeding still resolve normally; bots receive no geometry immunity. Both paired water busters now check the Water Resistance Down interaction; a new negative test removes the MT bot's invulnerability between the paired actions and requires Fluid Strike to kill it.
