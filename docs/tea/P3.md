# Alexander Prime: Mana implementation and verification

The public P3 strategy is `Mana — 3/4 fixed`. The existing Inception, Wormhole
and whole-phase practice boundaries share one encounter timeline and actor
infrastructure. Temporal Stasis remains its separate scenario. The inherited
NAUR movement is retained only through explicit strategy injection for
mechanics regression tests; it is not offered as another playable regional
strategy.

## Shared mechanics versus Mana decisions

| Shared encounter | Mana movement/assignment policy |
|---|---|
| Chastening Heat vulnerability and Divine Spear invulnerability checks, including bots | MT uses its job's invulnerability for the first set south at C; ST for the second |
| Four RNG-selected orb holders, actual homing/contact, overlap punishment, Heavy zones | Cross tethers and use separated Prime-side/side cardinal landing spots |
| Crystals drop at player positions; Heart must actually intersect four crystals | Drop a separated box on the heart side and regroup after the drops |
| Three live nearest-player Flarethrower baits, native turns and damage | MT center-west, ST heart-side, six-player group Prime-side |
| Restraining Order distance, Severity spreads, Solidarity stack, Heart-dependent Sacrament | Relative Heart north; tanks and Shared Sentence west, others east |
| Three nearest-unhit Alpha Swords and farthest-target Super Jump | Blue DPS north, H1 west, H2 south, MT east edge, ST and other DPS east |
| RNG numbers, Justice side and puddle layout; actual Sword/Charge collision, knockback and snapshots | Fixed 3 SW / 4 SE before Jump, both step outward afterward |
| Three actual Repentance soaks and failure when empty or incorrectly occupied | 56→78→12, odd west / even east |
| Two RNG DPS Enumeration targets, actual participant checks | Tanks north / healers south, marked DPS in D1→D2→D3→D4 priority |

`TeaManaP3Assignments` owns the regional endpoints and priorities.
`TeaManaP3AlexanderPrimeAi` supplies cohesive stage overrides to the common AI
movement/route scheduler. There are no Mana checks in the encounter timeline or
RNG state. A future Elemental strategy can supply its own movement stages while
retaining actors, casts, collision and failure logic. No Elemental behavior is
implemented or offered.

The metric coordinates inside Mana policy are safe-route implementation choices
around source-defined sectors, not recorded exact standing obligations. The
three flame sectors are explicitly separated for the current 90-degree model;
the six-player group remains on the Prime side and ST on the heart side. The
native flame width is inherited as unverified and still requires client checks.

Cast receipt agreement does not validate all target locks, jump landing frames,
visible omen lead times, animation startup or cleanup. The encounter code retains
its explicit cast, warp, facing, effect and damage events rather than collapsing
them into a single qualitative guide time. The two clears constrain the timeline,
but do not justify millisecond precision from the video narration.

The inherited post-Wormhole robot DPS window uses a scripted slow-clear kill
schedule for practice. It is not a player DPS simulator. Missing soaks, collision,
Heart/crystal failures and movement mistakes still resolve through their actual
mechanic checks; bots receive no success exemption.

Divine Spear, the final Divine Judgment and shatter damage explicitly opt into
bot mitigation checks. The shared AI presses the tank's actual job action
(Hallowed Ground, Holmgang, Living Dead or Superbolide) at 13.5 and 103.5 seconds,
(MT first, ST second), then uses an actual tank LB3 during the final countdown. Existing global human
mitigation preferences retain their meaning. A successful movement-only test
without these real statuses is insufficient for the bot tank and LB checks.

## Validation and remaining native checks

Dedicated bounded tests cover all four robot/puddle layouts, all eight player
seats across all eight Sacrament sectors, every Enumeration pair, the complete
phase, and ordinary Easy / eight-bot Hard runs. Eight cyclic number permutations
exercise each seat in every numbered responsibility. Negative cases remove a
soak, send a recently slashed player into the wrong soak, overlap the tank and
party flame baits, remove a bot's actual invulnerability, and strip tank LB3
before Divine Judgment. Additional lifecycle cases pause with live wormhole
objects, restart on the same seed and verify cleanup on Leave in ordinary and
spectator mode. The central Debug run passed all 32 dedicated cases, including strict bot
mitigation and lifecycle failures. Native presentation remains unverified.

In-game retest checklist:

* Both Prime orientations and all Heart destinations, actual gold-heart flight
  telegraph and Enigma Codex/status clearing.
* Three distinct flame baits, visible cone angle and separation, then the Mana
  Sword order and MT jump animation/landing.
* Both Justice sides with 3/4 fixed, both wormhole layouts, every number,
  chakram bait/flight, CC warp/facing and Charge/knockback presentation.
* Repentance native event-object beat/size changes, soak timing, real status
  markers and their cleanup between sizes.
* North/south Enumeration presentation, Mega Holy and tank LB3 presentation.
* Restart, Pause/Resume, Leave, offline/title-screen and inn arena layers.

Headless collision success cannot demonstrate that the client renders a jump,
ground omen or overhead marker. Record any client retest separately from the
Debug test result.

## Native playback feedback correction (2026-11-11)

The reviewed playback exposed an opener copied from the legacy north-facing
strategy. Mana now keeps the first invulnerability holder (MT) south at C and
the party north; ST takes the second set south. The legacy strategy keeps its
own tank order through shared AI policy methods. The guide at 04:27–04:52 is
explicit about south-facing Prime and MT's first invulnerability.

The flame review found a real resolution error: the old selector excluded a
player who had already baited. Local BossMod's `P3Inception2.Update` selects the
closest player on every bait, and its taken-player mask is advice rather than
an eligibility exclusion. FluffySim now selects the actual nearest living
player each time. MT and ST genuinely withdraw after their baits; the six-player
party remains in its Prime-side sector and naturally becomes closest for the
third flame. Holding the first bait spot can now bait a second flame and die to
Fire Resistance Down II. No bot immunity or completed-bait exclusion remains.

Withdrawal commands at 61.25 and 62.7 seconds are practice movement margins around
the existing flame lock/impact sequence, not newly measured retail timestamps.
Dedicated tests check both Prime orientations, tank distance before the third
bait, repeated-target failure, south-side tank ownership and real invulnerability
statuses. Native cone visibility and movement timing still need another playback.

Correction-pass Debug verification passed all 35 dedicated Mana P3 cases and
all 89 legacy encounter cases. This includes genuine repeated-bait failure,
both-orientation withdrawals and the changed MT/ST invulnerability responsibilities.
