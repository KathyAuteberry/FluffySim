# Brute Justice and Cruise Chaser — Mana

## Assignment authority

The [Japanese macro](https://jp.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/34120564/blog/5274750/) is the assignment authority, compared with the [English translation](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/40730370/blog/5524788/) and [Nukemaru P2 guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yuLjtpm2ti8). The full Japanese automatic captions and a 360p guide copy were inspected in temporary storage. Guide chapters at 03:02, 06:13, 10:08 and 14:34 describe the four principal blocks. Frames around 04:06, 10:30–12:10 and 14:20–15:30 show routes and Nisi lineups. Guide chapter times are not encounter timestamps.

The macro specifies **four transfers**, rather than the task scaffold's three pass cycles. First and second pairs are MT–D1, ST–D2, H1–D3, H2–D4. DPS move counterclockwise for the first pass; D2 and ST finish Spin Crusher first. The third-pass line, north to south, is MT, D1, D3, D4, D2, ST; healers resolve their matched transfer before tanks. The fourth lineup, away from the ice, is ST, H2, H1, MT, with supports establishing positions first and DPS joining their matching Final Judgment. The old guide illustrates healer movement in its third-pass examples; the newer macro's healer-first priority takes precedence.

Water and lightning assignments are explicit in `TeaManaP2Assignments`:

| Round | Water | Lightning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First marked healer and three non-lightning DPS, at 4 | First marked DPS and MT, north |
| 2 | All four DPS, at 2 | MT and H1, north |
| 3 | ST and three DPS without Water Resistance Down, at B | Both healers, at D |

Enumeration uses west-to-east DPS priority D3, D4, D2, D1; the western marked DPS shares with H1/H2, and the eastern marked DPS shares with the other two DPS. Mines are baited by D3 at five o'clock and ST at six. The English macro's D3 southwest direction conflicts with the Japanese five-o'clock assignment; this implementation follows Japanese southeast. D3 baits Super Jump.

## Recordings and discrepancies

Both clears in TEA-L01 corroborate the compression role groups and healer-first third transfer. In pull 4, the third transfers reach SCH then WHM before PLD/DRK; job labels here identify roles without retaining player identities. The first three initial transfers match MT–D1, H1–D3 and H2–D4; the delayed ST–D2 transfer follows Spin Crusher.

The captured **third water is near outer marker 2**, with participant X approximately 111–118, despite the macro calling for inner B. Native replay waymarks place B near X=105.8 and marker 2 near X=113.7. This is a real source difference, not a reason to rename the marks. The Mana candidate follows the macro's inner B. The capture remains timing and native-event evidence; its routes are not copied wholesale as proof of the exact selected convention.

Walking coordinates are practical placements within the documented sectors. They are not claimed to be frame-perfect retail paths. Nisi is passed by actual touching hitboxes; the AI waits for observed matching statuses before leaving a pass. There is no direct application of a successful Nisi assignment by the AI.

During the third transfers and Gavel staging, bot-only movement steering follows live carrier positions rather than trusting old route predictions. Different active letters remain obstacles even when their final judgments match. A deliberate transfer allows contact while due, then removes that permission once the matching statuses appear. A conservative steering boundary does not replace the arena's ordinary fall check. Human input and native forced movement remain independent; movement fields are released by the existing party/run cleanup and explicitly reset on initialization. Third-water vulnerable players begin leaving after their real third transfer completes, and stale return callbacks cannot replace a newer stack route. These are practice bot routes, not measured retail movement timestamps.

## Shared mechanics and extension points

Shared scenario/state own the actor lifecycle, cast schedule, real action/status IDs, collision, transfer timers, compressed-element handoffs, puddles, mines, ice/gaol, shield, boss attacks and Gavel failure. The Mana AI owns positions, role priorities, pass routes, mine support and numbered convention choices. `TeaManaP2Assignments` isolates the role decisions. A future Elemental AI can consume the same state without copying the timeline. Scenario identity remains `BJ/CC`, with the explicit Mana AI group providing strategy identity; Elemental is not exposed.

The inherited non-Mana choreography is retained only in test-side `TeaP2BjCcLegacyAi` and its explicit scenario factory for the original negative mechanics tests. It is not playable from the encounter catalog. This avoids relabeling old NAUR routes as Mana.

Known extension limits: the shared scenario currently starts Brute Justice on MT and Cruise Chaser on ST rather than a complete enmity model. A future strategy requiring different ownership or mid-phase Provoke behavior will need that specific extension. The current full-party shield drain is an approximation based on frontal participants, not a complete attack/DPS simulation. Exact Photon HP, healing and damage rotations are not simulated.

## Mitigation and presentation limits

Mines check real mitigation statuses on every seat, including bots. Both tanks also schedule their job's short defensive before sharing Double Rocket Punch; its two-tank geometry remains enforced. The inherited 36% minimum is explicitly an **unverified practice threshold** until the package establishes pre-mitigation mine damage. Bots schedule Rampart and their job's major cooldown. Existing local action mappings may present upgraded defensive statuses for synced actions; correct level-80 defensive icons and values require a separate native check. These limitations are not masked by granting bots immunity.

The Nisi contact-radius model remains **unverified**. It uses summed player hitbox radii (normally about one yalm). Fresh native movement poses at two captured status gains were approximately 1.67 and 1.91 yalms apart; status/pose arrival and players moving apart prevent deriving an exact server contact boundary from these samples. The practice radius must be retested rather than treated as a proven retail distance.

Native checks remain necessary for both boss models/animation states, real overhead Nisi and Final Judgment icons, compressed markers, missile/mine snapshots, ice growth, gaol line of sight, Plasma Shield facing, Super Jump visibility, Apocalyptic Ray direction and hit timing, cast bars and enemy list, then Restart/Pause/Leave and offline/inn initialization. Headless success cannot establish those visuals.
